AssistantVoiceRecorder is a local-first meeting recorder for Android.

Choose a folder for recordings, then select AssistantVoiceRecorder as your
Digital Assistant. Invoking the assistant starts a microphone recording and
shows a persistent notification while it is active.

Recordings are stored in your selected folder as M4A audio files. You can play
them back at different speeds and delete them from the app. Optional uploads
can send recording metadata and audio to a server that you configure yourself.

The app cannot record protected phone-call audio. If a call is using the
microphone, it stops rather than creating a silent recording.
